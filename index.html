Locally, change is accelerating.

A community sees a shoreline retreating, a road, home, or cemetary collapsing into the sea.

But without repeatable measurements, it's difficult to quantify how quickly the landscape is changing, or know exactly when action is needed.

Across Canada, the problem is everywhere.

From the Arctic to the Atlantic, Pacific, and Great Lakes, Canadian communities face erosion and flooding across an enormous and difficult-to-monitor geography.

But we don't measure change everywhere.

Satellites

✓ Wide Coverage
✕ Often too coarse for local change

Professional Surveys

✓ High Precision
✕ Expensive and infrequent

Community Observation

✓ Local and Immediate
✕ Difficult to standardize

The result: vast areas sit in the dark between expensive professional surveys and coarse regional datasets.

And Canada is only the beginning.

Across the world, communities, infrastructure, and ecosystems are being reshaped by coastal and riverine change.

FluxAtlas connects these scales.

From smartphone surveys to professional-grade terrain data, FluxAtlas turns fragmented observations into a continuous, high-resolution record of landscape change.

Built on Research. Designed for Practice.

Academic Research

FluxAtlas originated from research into community-based coastal monitoring at the University of British Columbia and has grown into a platform for making repeat, community based, environmental monitoring more accessible.

Field Validation

Designed from firsthand experience conducting coastal monitoring in some of Canada's most challenging environments. Developed and tested across diverse environments including the Arctic, Coastal BC, and the Great Lakes.

Professional Practice

Developed directly by practicing coastal engineers to solve real-world industry bottlenecks.

Community Focused

Built to empower all users, not just the GIS wizards, to use terrain data to make the decisions that matter.
